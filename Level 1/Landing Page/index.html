Cosmos

A Journey Through the Universe

The universe is a vast, mysterious space with countless stars, planets, and
secrets waiting to be discovered.

Earth

Earth is our home, full of life, plants, animals, and people. It's the only planet we know where life exists!

Sun

The Sun gives us light and warmth, making life possible on Earth. It’s a giant ball of hot gases at the center of our solar system!

Milky Way

The Milky Way is the galaxy we live in, filled with billions of stars and planets. It appears as and glowing band in the night sky!

Black Hole

A black hole has intense gravity that traps everything, including light. It forms when a massive star collapses at the end of its life.
